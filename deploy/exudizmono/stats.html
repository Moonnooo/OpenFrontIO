A CLEARER MEASURE OF SKILL

Every rating has a place.

Exudizmono skill ratings

Results over playtime.

Each mode has its own skill rating, starting at 1000. Ratings change after completed public games based on opponents and results. FFA also uses finishing positions when recorded. Equal displayed ratings share a leaderboard rank.

Earn a reliable rank.

Your first 10 rated matches in each mode are provisional. Private, solo, cancelled, cheat-enabled and humans-versus-nations games do not affect competitive ratings. Games missing human identities or a clear winner remain unrated.

Fairer matches, next.

Skill-based matchmaking is not enabled yet. The 1v1 and 2v2 ladders will fill when those queues produce completed games. All multiplayer and all-team views show the match-weighted average of separate mode ratings.
